# LOVE Formula - Architectural Positioning

Elias Systems Ltd | 11 October 2026 | Version 0.1
Publication scope: Public architectural positioning — bounded evidence, no protected implementation disclosed

The LOVE Formula is the ethical foundation informing the independently developed sovereign AI governance architecture of Elias Systems.

Elias does not need to create its own AI model to govern intelligence. Its architecture separates intelligence from governing authority and conditional execution.

This document explains those architectural relationships without disclosing the protected formula or its proprietary implementation.

## 1. Historical Foundation

The LOVE Formula predates the current Elias AI governance implementations.

The September 2026 WP02 examination preserved a bounded ethical principle: failure of one required condition cannot be compensated for by success in another.

Numerical approval alone does not establish final governing authority. Human review remains required within the WP02 framework.

WP02 did not establish objective moral truth, independent validation of its underlying metrics, or present execution authority.

Later Elias systems must establish their own capabilities through independent implementation, examination and evidence.

The historical WP02 record remains preserved and unchanged.

## 2. Sovereign Governance Architecture

Elias Systems develops an independently designed, deterministic, offline-capable AI governance architecture.

The architecture is not a foundation AI model. It is a separate computing and governance framework designed to establish the conditions under which intelligence may operate and consequential actions may proceed.

The LOVE Formula informs the ethical intention behind this architecture. Its protected formulation and proprietary implementation are not disclosed here.

The wider Elias architecture separates identity, observation, understanding, governance, intelligence, execution and witness evidence into distinct responsibilities.

Governance can operate before an AI model is invoked, at execution boundaries and during evidence recording.

AI capability does not automatically establish execution authority. Permission to act remains conditional on applicable authority, scope, state and governance requirements.

Defined governance decisions can be deterministic without requiring the underlying language model to be deterministic.

The architecture is designed for use with different intelligence providers, subject to integration and verification.

Offline-capable governance does not imply that every AI model, application function or infrastructure dependency currently operates offline.

Individual implementations and security properties require their own testing and evidence.

## 3. The Elias Ecosystem

The LOVE Formula informs a connected family of Elias Systems developments. Each implementation has its own technical responsibilities and evidence boundaries.

### Elias EIE - Live Pilot

EIE is a deployed conversational AI application undergoing live pilot testing. It provides a practical environment for developing governed interactions, memory controls, provider integration and user experience.

### High Precision Mode (HPM)

HPM is a specialised interaction mode designed to support greater precision, explicit boundaries and careful handling of uncertainty.

### Emotional Programming Layer (EPL)

EPL is an AI-output and interaction governance contract informed by the LOVE Formula’s defined qualities, principles and constraints. It guides non-diagnostic model responses without claiming human emotional understanding; model-level guidance is distinct from independently implemented and tested enforcement controls.

### Memory Governance

EIE includes mechanisms for managing remembered information and proposed memories. Information being offered to a system does not automatically make it authoritative.

### Deterministic Execution Governance

Elias governance components examine identity, current authority, scope, state and execution eligibility separately from model-generated intelligence.

### CCI-8 Cybersecurity Examination

The eight-claim programme examines specific compromise-containment and integrity properties. Claim 5 remains OPEN. Historical failures and bounded successful results are preserved without retrospective alteration.

### Governed Offline Knowledge - Planned Development

A proposed separate knowledge library would allow approved reference information to remain available offline, independently of conversational memory and the language model. Its implementation and assurance remain future work.

### Reusable Commercial Architecture

These components provide a foundation for scoped customer implementations, governance integrations and potential white-label applications. Customer-specific production readiness and assurance require further verification.

The existence of a shared ethical foundation does not automatically prove the correctness, security or effectiveness of every implementation.

## 4. Ethical Intention and Governing Authority

The LOVE Formula informs the ethical purpose of Elias governance. It is not itself an unconditional permission to execute.

Intelligence can interpret, recommend or propose. Governing authority must be established separately.

Elias components are designed to examine identity, applicable rules, present state, permitted scope and execution conditions.

Where required authority cannot be established, a governed system may withhold, deny or escalate an action.

Human authority remains an explicit design concern. The original WP02 examination also preserved its own human-review requirement.

These mechanisms are implemented and verified at the level of individual components and bounded tests, not assumed to apply universally.

This separation is central to the architectural sovereignty pursued by Elias Systems.

## 5. Commercial Applications and Licensing

Elias Systems is developing reusable governance infrastructure for AI applications, enterprise integrations and white-label solutions.

The LOVE Formula informs the ethical foundation while Elias governance components provide defined technical boundaries.

Potential applications include governed AI assistants, organisational knowledge systems, memory governance, execution approval, change-control workflows and security enforcement.

Customers may require different AI models, interfaces, knowledge sources and operating environments without necessarily changing the underlying Elias governance principles.

The intended commercial model supports licensing and scoped implementation while Elias retains ownership of its underlying intellectual property.

Current capabilities provide a basis for demonstrations, pilots and defined customer projects.

Enterprise readiness, security assurance, deployment compatibility and contractual requirements must be assessed for each implementation.

No customer deployment is represented as independently certified merely because it uses Elias components.

The protected LOVE Formula and proprietary governance implementation are not disclosed by this document.

## 6. Current Evidence and Future Direction

Status as of 11 October 2026.

### Implemented and Operational

Elias EIE has been deployed and is undergoing live pilot testing.

The application incorporates Standard, High Precision Mode (HPM) and Emotional Programming Layer (EPL) interaction modes.

EIE includes provider integration, conversational functionality and developing memory governance capabilities.

Elias has developed independent deterministic governance components and runtime implementations that can be tested separately from the underlying language model.

### Controlled Security Examination

The CCI-8 programme examines eight defined compromise-containment and integrity claims.

Claim 5 remains OPEN.

Test 004A is preserved as a historical failure.

Tests 004B, 004C and 004D have recorded bounded PASS results within their stated test conditions.

Test 004E is in preparation and has not yet been executed.

These results do not establish complete control-plane security or universal enforcement across every deployment.

### Current Limitations

EIE remains a pilot application undergoing reliability, security and operational improvements.

The deployed application currently depends on external hosting and AI services.

Offline-capable deterministic governance does not constitute a fully offline AI application.

Historical LOVE Formula evidence does not automatically establish the validity of later implementations.

### Future Direction

Continue the eight-claim cybersecurity examination without presuming future results.

Further develop reusable governance interfaces, customer integration pathways and white-label deployment options.

Examine a governed offline knowledge library for approved reference information independent of the language model.

Strengthen deployment reliability, security verification, evidence continuity and operational independence.

All future capabilities remain subject to implementation, testing and appropriate review.
