# Elias LOVE Formula - Sovereign AI Governance Architecture

Elias Systems Ltd | 11 October 2026 | Public architecture overview

## Overview

The LOVE Formula provides the ethical foundation informing Elias Systems' independently developed AI governance architecture.

Elias develops deterministic, offline-capable governance components that operate separately from the underlying intelligence provider.

The architecture distinguishes intelligence, governing authority, conditional execution and witness evidence.

## Practical Development

The wider Elias ecosystem includes EIE, HPM, EPL, memory governance and execution-control components. EPL is an AI-output and interaction governance contract informed by the LOVE Formula; its model-level guidance must not be confused with independently enforced and tested controls.

EIE is currently undergoing live pilot testing.

The CCI-8 security programme remains in progress, with Claim 5 OPEN.

## Evidence and Limitations

Historical LOVE Formula examinations and later implementations retain separate evidence boundaries.

Offline governance does not imply that the complete AI application operates offline.
